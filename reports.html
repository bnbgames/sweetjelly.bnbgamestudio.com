      Win rate and players stopped, by level

      win rate = wins ÷ (wins + fails)

      Players stopped = cloud saves whose next unbeaten level is this one. Inactive = no cloud save for 7+ days, i.e. they likely quit here. Wins count every win, including replays. Fails count only when the player gives up (declines to continue).
