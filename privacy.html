Information Collection and Use

The game stores most player data (game progression, in-game currency, preferences) locally on your device. We do not transmit this information to our own servers.

The game also integrates with third-party services that may collect information used to identify you or your device for the purposes of showing ads, tracking analytics, providing cloud-saved progression, and enabling leaderboards and achievements. Those services are listed below.

Google Account and Cloud-Saved Games

If you choose to sign in with your Google account, the game uses Google Play Games Services to provide leaderboards, achievements, and cross-device cloud-saved progression. When you sign in, the game accesses:

  • Your Google Play Games player ID and display name — used to identify your entries on leaderboards and to display your name in achievement notifications.
  • The Google Drive App Data folder (drive.appdata scope) — used exclusively to store your game progression (highest level reached, stars earned, scores) in a private folder on your Google Drive that is only accessible by this app. We do not read or write any other part of your Google Drive. We cannot see your other files, photos, or documents.

This cloud-saved data allows you to reinstall the game or switch to a new device without losing your progress.

Signing in with Google is optional. The game functions fully without it using only local device storage.

Third-Party Services

The game uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Play Services — sign-in, leaderboards, achievements, and cloud-saved games. (Privacy policy)
  • Google AdMob — interstitial and rewarded video ads, with mediation to additional ad networks (see below). (Privacy policy)
  • Firebase (Analytics, Cloud Messaging, Realtime Database, Authentication) — usage analytics, push notifications, and anonymous gameplay statistics. (Privacy policy)
  • Unity — game engine and Unity services telemetry. (Privacy policy)

Advertising and Ad Mediation Networks

Ads in the game are served via Google AdMob with mediation. Mediation allows additional ad networks to bid on each ad request so the best-performing ad is shown. Each mediation partner may collect device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID), IP address, and limited device characteristics to deliver and measure ads. The currently integrated mediation partners are:

  • AppLovin — Privacy policy
  • Mintegral — Privacy policy
  • Pangle (ByteDance) — Privacy policy
  • Unity Ads — Privacy policy
  • Liftoff Monetize (formerly Vungle) — Privacy policy

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland we display a consent dialog on first launch using Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). Your consent choices are transmitted to mediation partners via the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework (TCF v2.2) signal. You can change your choice at any time from the game's settings.

Log Data

Whenever you use our Service, in case of a crash or other error, we (through the third-party products listed above) may collect data and information called Log Data. Log Data may include your device's Internet Protocol ("IP") address, device model, operating system version, the configuration of the game during use, the time and date of your use of the Service, and other statistics.

Cookies and Similar Identifiers

The mobile game itself does not use browser cookies. Third-party SDKs embedded in the game may use device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID) for the purposes described above. You can reset your Advertising ID at any time from your device settings.

Data Retention and Deletion

  • On-device data (progression, currency, preferences) is retained until you uninstall the game or clear the app's storage from your device settings.
  • Cloud-saved data in the Google Drive App Data folder is retained as long as the game is installed against your Google account. To delete it, visit https://myaccount.google.com/permissions, find “Sweet Jelly Match 3 Puzzle”, and choose “Remove access”. This revokes our access and deletes the data stored in the App Data folder.
  • Leaderboard and achievement data stored on Google's servers can be removed through Google Play Games. Open the Play Games app, go to Settings → Delete Play Games account & data, and follow the instructions.
  • Analytics and ad measurement data handled by Google, Firebase, and the mediation partners is retained per their own privacy policies linked above.
  • For any data deletion request not covered above, email support@bnbgamestudio.com and we will respond within 30 days.
