Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-28

B&B Game Studio ("we", "our", "us") built the Sweet Jelly Match 3 Puzzle game for iOS and Android as a free, ad-supported game with optional in-app purchases. This policy explains what information we collect, how it is used, and what choices you have. It applies to both the iOS (App Store) and Android (Google Play) versions of the game unless a section says otherwise.

By using the Service you agree to the collection and use of information described in this policy. We do not use or share your information except as described here.

Information Collection and Use

The game stores your game data (progression, in-game currency, preferences) locally on your device. When you are online, the game also backs up your progress to a cloud database hosted for us by Google Firebase, so that it can be restored if you reinstall the game or change device (see Game Account, Cloud Save and Leaderboards below). We do not ask for your name, email address, or phone number.

The game also integrates with third-party services that may collect information used to identify you or your device for the purposes of showing ads, measuring analytics, processing purchases, sending notifications, and providing sign-in, leaderboards, and achievements. Those services are listed below.

Game Account, Cloud Save and Leaderboards

Anonymous game account

On first launch the game creates an anonymous game account using Firebase Authentication. This account is identified only by a random ID; it is not linked to your name or email. It is used to keep your cloud save and leaderboard entries together.

Optional sign-in with Google Play Games (Android) or Game Center (iOS)

The game offers sign-in with the game service of your platform:

  • Android — Google Play Games Services. If you sign in, the game accesses your Google Play Games player ID and display name, and uses Google Play Games for achievements.
  • iOS — Apple Game Center. If you are signed in to Game Center, the game receives your Game Center player ID and a verification credential from Apple.

When you sign in, that player ID is linked to your anonymous game account so your progress follows you across reinstalls and devices. We do not receive your Google or Apple account password or email address. Signing in is optional; the game works fully without it.

Cloud save

Your cloud save contains your game progress: levels reached, stars and scores per level, in-game collection progress (stickers, packs and similar items), and your leaderboard nickname, if you set one. It is stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) and can only be read by your own game account.

Android only: earlier versions of the game stored progress in the Google Drive App Data folder (drive.appdata scope) through Google Play Games saved games. If you sign in with Google, the game reads that saved game once to move your progress to the new cloud save. It only accesses a private folder on your Google Drive that is only accessible by this app. We cannot see your other files, photos, or documents.

Leaderboards

The game has public leaderboards (level reached, total stars, best score). Your entry shows your leaderboard nickname and score to all players. If you have not chosen a nickname, a generated name such as “Player1234” is shown. Please do not use your real name or other personal information as your nickname. Offensive nicknames are filtered and may be removed.

In-App Purchases

The game offers optional in-app purchases of virtual items. Payments are processed by the Apple App Store (iOS) or Google Play (Android) under their own terms and privacy policies. We never receive your card number or other payment details. We receive only transaction information (such as the product purchased and a receipt) so that we can deliver the item to you.

Push Notifications

The game can send push notifications (for example, reminders and event announcements) using Firebase Cloud Messaging, which on iOS is delivered through the Apple Push Notification service. The game asks for your permission before sending notifications. You can turn them off at any time in your device's notification settings.

Third-Party Services

The game uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Play Services (Android) — Google Play Games sign-in, achievements, legacy saved games, and in-app purchases. (Privacy policy)
  • Apple Game Center and App Store (iOS) — Game Center sign-in and in-app purchases. (Privacy policy)
  • Google AdMob — interstitial and rewarded video ads, with mediation to additional ad networks (see below). (Privacy policy)
  • Firebase (Analytics, Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Realtime Database, Cloud Messaging) — usage analytics, the anonymous game account, cloud save, leaderboards, anonymous gameplay statistics, and push notifications. (Privacy policy)
  • Unity — game engine, Unity In-App Purchasing, and Unity services telemetry. (Privacy policy)

Advertising and Ad Mediation Networks

Ads in the game are served via Google AdMob with mediation. Mediation allows additional ad networks to bid on each ad request so the best-performing ad is shown. Each mediation partner may collect device identifiers (the Android Advertising ID or, on iOS, the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) if you allow tracking), IP address, and limited device characteristics to deliver and measure ads. The currently integrated mediation partners are:

  • AppLovin — Privacy policy
  • Mintegral — Privacy policy
  • Pangle (ByteDance) — Privacy policy
  • Unity Ads — Privacy policy
  • Liftoff Monetize (formerly Vungle) — Privacy policy

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland we display a consent dialog on first launch using Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). Your consent choices are transmitted to mediation partners via the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework (TCF v2.2) signal. You can change your choice at any time from the game's settings.

App Tracking Transparency (iOS)

On iOS 14.5 and later, the game uses Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt to ask whether you allow it to track you across apps and websites owned by other companies. If you allow tracking, the IDFA may be used by AdMob and the mediation partners listed above to show personalised ads and measure ad performance. If you do not allow it, the IDFA is not shared and you will still see ads, but they will be less relevant to you. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Log Data

Whenever you use our Service, in case of a crash or other error, we (through the third-party products listed above) may collect data and information called Log Data. Log Data may include your device's Internet Protocol ("IP") address, device model, operating system version, the configuration of the game during use, the time and date of your use of the Service, and other statistics.

Cookies and Similar Identifiers

The mobile game itself does not use browser cookies. Third-party SDKs embedded in the game may use device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID, or the IDFA on iOS if you allow tracking) for the purposes described above. On Android you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device's Google settings. On iOS you can allow or deny tracking for each app in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Data Retention and Deletion

  • On-device data (progression, currency, preferences) is retained until you uninstall the game or clear the app's storage from your device settings.
  • Cloud save and leaderboard entries stored in Firebase are kept while your game account exists. To have them deleted, email support@bnbgamestudio.com with your leaderboard nickname and platform (iOS or Android). We will delete your game account, cloud save, and leaderboard entries within 30 days.
  • Android — Google Drive App Data folder: to delete legacy saved-game data, visit https://myaccount.google.com/permissions, find “Sweet Jelly Match 3 Puzzle”, and choose “Remove access”. This revokes our access and deletes the data stored in the App Data folder.
  • Android — Google Play Games achievements are stored on Google's servers and can be removed through Google Play Games. Open the Play Games app, go to Settings → Delete Play Games account & data, and follow the instructions.
  • iOS — Game Center: your Game Center account is managed by Apple. You can sign out of Game Center in Settings → Game Center, and manage your Apple account data at https://privacy.apple.com/.
  • Analytics and ad measurement data handled by Google, Firebase, Unity, and the mediation partners is retained per their own privacy policies linked above.
  • For any data deletion request not covered above, email support@bnbgamestudio.com and we will respond within 30 days.
